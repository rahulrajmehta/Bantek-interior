Our Story

Enriching Ranchi's Architectural Landscape

Rooted at Itki Road in Ranchi, Bantek Interior was established with a singular vision: to liberate homeowners from the stress of uncoordinated contractors, sub-standard materials, and unpredictable timelines.

We believe an interior is not just decoration—it is the backdrop to your daily life. Guided by the principle of "marrying aesthetics with functionality," we blend high-grade engineering with warm, human-centric design aesthetics, using only genuine marine-grade plywood, European hardware, and artisanal finishes.

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide free site visits in Ranchi?

Yes! Our senior design team conducts complimentary in-person site measurements and requirement discussions across Ranchi (Bazra, Itki Rd, Morabadi, Bariatu, Kanke, Harmu, Doranda, Lalpur, Ashok Nagar).

How long does a complete home interior take to complete?

For a standard 2 or 3 BHK turnkey interior, we guarantee delivery within 45 working days following 3D design sign-off, backed by strict penalty-protected milestones managed by Bahadur Kumar Modi.

What materials and warranties do you offer?

We use exclusively IS:710 boiling waterproof (BWP) marine grade plywood, Hettich / Hafele soft-closing German hardware, and offer a comprehensive 10-year warranty against manufacturing defects and termite issues.
